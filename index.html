What the data tells you about your content mix

🎬 Video is your highest-ROI format
13% engagement rate and 10.3% CTR — the best numbers across all formats. Yet only 19% of posts are video. Every video post drives 2.65× more engagement than a text post for the same effort.
🖼 Image is your workhorse
39 image posts generate 56.7K total impressions — the biggest reach engine. Welcome aboard, Employee Milestone, and Employer Branding posts are all image-based and consistently hit 1,400+ impressions each.

Lowest avg impressions (1,038) and lowest engagement (4.9%). But the Jan 23 text post hit 4,802 impressions — suggesting that highly resonant thought leadership topics can break through regardless of format.

Only 5 articles posted (Apr–Jun 2026) — too early to judge. The Stanford AI Index series averaged 614 impressions with 5.5% engagement. Worth continuing to test for HASQ audience nurturing.

Campaign Insights & Recommendations

What's Working

Signals from campaign data

📈 MOF outperforms TOF
HASQ-MOF-May (0.391% CTR, $14.58 CPC) beat TOF-April (0.286%, $23.81 CPC). Retargeting warm audiences is more efficient — prioritise MOF budget in next cycle.
🎬 Video reach at ultra-low CPM
ML Boost Video delivered 79K impressions at just $1.88 CPM via Audience Network. 81.8% video view rate signals strong content quality. Use for brand awareness at scale before HASQ lead campaigns.
🌍 North America audience confirmed
US (36.9%) + Canada (12.8%) = 49.7% of ad impressions — confirming HASQ geo-targeting is working. Greater Toronto Area alone drove 19 clicks — highest single-city engagement.
👔 C-Suite is in your audience
Owner, Founder, President, CEO = top 4 job titles reached. Senior-level targeting is working — these are HASQ's ideal buyers.

Optimisation Recommendations

Next campaign cycle

💰 Budget: Shift to MOF-heavy (60/40)
MOF delivers 37% lower CPC than TOF. For the next $1,500, allocate $900 to MOF retargeting and $600 to TOF awareness.
🎯 Add Lead Gen forms to HASQ campaigns
Current campaigns drive clicks to the website but track 0 leads/conversions in the report. Adding LinkedIn Lead Gen forms directly to HASQ ads would capture contacts without a landing page dependency.
📢 Run Follower Ads alongside HASQ
Follower Ads targeting US/Canada professionals in Engineering, L&D, and Operations verticals would compound HASQ brand awareness while growing the company page to international audiences.
🔁 Test A/B on ad creative for HASQ
TOF Set 01 vs Set 02 had near-identical CTR — suggests audience difference, not creative. Test 1 image ad vs 1 video ad in the same audience to find the format that resonates best for HASQ.
